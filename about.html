About Youth & Sports Empower

Dedicated to nurturing youth talent, promoting sports excellence, and creating pathways for holistic development across India

Our Mission

To create an ecosystem where every young Indian has access to quality sports infrastructure, training, and opportunities for personal growth, fostering a culture of excellence and national pride through sports and youth development programs.

Our Vision

To establish India as a sporting powerhouse on the global stage while empowering youth to become catalysts for positive social change, contributing to the nation's progress through their energy, ideas, and leadership.

Our Journey

Foundation

1950

Establishment of the Ministry of Youth Affairs to channel the energy of independent India's youth towards nation-building activities.

Sports Integration

1982

Merger with Sports department after the successful Asian Games in Delhi, expanding focus to include athletic development.

National Youth Policy

1988

Introduction of the first comprehensive National Youth Policy to provide a framework for youth engagement and development.

Sports Authority of India

1994

Strengthening of the Sports Authority of India as the nodal agency for athletic training and infrastructure development.

Youth Connect Initiative

2008

Launch of youth-focused digital platforms to engage the next generation through technology and social media.

Khelo India Launch

2018

Introduction of the flagship Khelo India program to revitalize the sports culture at the grassroots level across the country.

Present Day

2023

Continued expansion of programs with focus on digital integration, international collaboration, and preparing for global sporting events.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the main objective of Youth & Sports Empower?

Our primary objective is to develop both youth and sports in India by creating comprehensive programs that provide opportunities for training, competition, and personal development. We aim to harness the potential of India's youth population while fostering sporting excellence at national and international levels.

How can young athletes access government sports schemes?

Young athletes can access government sports schemes through multiple channels including:

  • School sports programs affiliated with our initiatives
  • District and state-level sports authorities
  • Sports Authority of India training centers
  • Direct applications through our website for specific schemes
  • Participation in Khelo India games and talent identification events

What youth volunteer opportunities are available?

We offer several volunteer programs including the National Youth Corps, National Service Scheme (NSS), and event-specific volunteering opportunities. These programs focus on community development, social service, environmental conservation, disaster management, and sports event organization. Volunteers gain valuable leadership experience while contributing to national development goals.

How are sports funds allocated to different disciplines?

Sports funding is allocated based on a strategic framework that considers multiple factors:

  • Priority disciplines identified in the National Sports Policy
  • Medal potential in international competitions
  • Grassroots participation and popularity
  • Infrastructure requirements and maintenance costs
  • Coach development and training needs
  • Historical performance and growth potential

Additionally, special allocations are made for para-sports, women's sports, and traditional/indigenous sports to ensure inclusive development.

Can international athletes participate in Khelo India programs?

Khelo India programs are primarily designed for Indian citizens to develop domestic sporting talent. However, we do organize select international competitions and exchange programs where foreign athletes can participate. For training collaborations, we have special provisions for joint camps and knowledge exchange with international sporting bodies and academies.

How does the ministry support rural sports development?

Rural sports development is a key priority, supported through:

  • The Panchayat Yuva Krida aur Khel Abhiyan (PYKKA) - developing playfields at village levels
  • Mobile coaching teams that visit remote areas
  • Special talent identification camps in rural districts
  • Rural competitions feeding into state and national level events
  • Training of local coaches and physical education teachers
  • Provision of basic equipment to rural schools and community centers
